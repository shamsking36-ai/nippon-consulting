Our Specialized Consulting Services

Structural Design of RCC Building

Seismic-resistant RCC framing, foundation design, bar bending schedules (BBS), and Good-For-Construction (GFC) structural drawings per IS 456 & IS 1893.

02

Architectural Plan of Building

Functional floor plans, municipal submission drawings, elevations, cross-sections, and space-optimized layouts for residential & commercial buildings.

03

Cost Estimation of Building Project

Item-wise Bill of Quantities (BOQ), DSR/SOR rate analysis, material takeoff, and bank loan/tender valuation estimates with high accuracy.

04

Highway Project

Geometric alignment, pavement design, culverts, minor/major bridges, retaining walls, and drainage consultancy per IRC & MoRTH standards.

05

Tunnel

Tunnel cross-section quantity assessment, portal structures, rock support BOQ, lining estimation, and feasibility evaluation for road & rail corridors.

06

Railway Projects

Formation earthwork, ROB/RUB structures, railway bridges, station building engineering, and track-bed quantity & cost consultancy.

DPR Preparation of Building Projects

Complete Detailed Project Reports (DPR) integrating architectural layouts, structural concepts, abstract of cost, specifications, and implementation schedules.
